Our Story

The Foundation

Why MaatriSparsh exists

In India, childbirth is celebrated with great joy — but the period that follows, those critical postpartum weeks when a mother's body and mind are at their most vulnerable, is often left without structured clinical support.

Existing hospital stays focus on the newborn's needs. Mothers receive discharge instructions, but rarely the hands-on, evidence-based recovery care that modern obstetric science clearly supports.

"We saw mothers leaving hospitals still in pain, still anxious, still unsure of how to feed their babies — and we knew something had to change."

— MaatriSparsh Founders

MaatriSparsh was built to bridge that gap. We partner directly with hospitals and maternity units to deliver certified postpartum massage therapy and holistic wellness services — inside the ward, from Day 1 of admission.

Our programmes are grounded in peer-reviewed research, designed for Indian hospital contexts, and delivered by certified therapists trained in postpartum physiology and clinical protocol adherence.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Every decision we make — every protocol we design, every therapist we train — is guided by these principles.

01
Clinical Rigour

Every service we offer is grounded in peer-reviewed evidence and physician-approved protocols. We do not compromise on clinical standards — ever.

02
Compassionate Presence

Healing requires more than technique. Our therapists are trained to bring warmth, patience, and emotional sensitivity to every session — treating the whole mother, not just her body.

03
Dignity in Recovery

We believe every mother, regardless of birth type or circumstance, deserves to feel cared for, seen, and honoured during her recovery.

04
Partnership First

We exist to support your hospital, not compete with it. Our model is built on transparent, measurable, and seamless integration into your care framework.

05
Cultural Rootedness

We draw deeply from India's own healing traditions — Ayurveda, marma therapy, and ancestral postpartum wisdom — blending the timeless with the evidence-based.

06
Continuous Learning

The science of postpartum care is evolving. We invest in ongoing therapist training, clinical research updates, and outcome measurement to remain at the frontier.
