Our Core Programmes

Carefully designed for every birth type — from Day 1 through discharge. Each programme is personalised by our certified therapists following an initial assessment.

Post Caesarean Section
LSCS Recovery Programme

A carefully graduated programme beginning from Day 2 post-surgery, respecting wound healing timelines while progressively restoring circulation and comfort.

What's Included
  • Day 1: Full clinical assessment — delivery type, vitals, physician notes reviewed. Personalised care plan created.
  • Day 2: Gentle full body massage — slow pace, low pressure. Foot, back, head, shoulder, neck, hand.
  • Day 3: Progressive full body massage — mid pace, medium pressure. Lymphatic drainage begins.
  • Day 4+: Complete wellness session — full body massage + spa treatment including pedicure.
