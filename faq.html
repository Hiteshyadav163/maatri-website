General Questions

MaatriSparsh is a certified postpartum wellness programme that brings evidence-based massage therapy and holistic spa care directly to hospital maternity wards. We partner with hospitals to deliver in-ward recovery services for new mothers — from Day 1 of admission through discharge.

Our name combines "Maatri" (mother, in Sanskrit) and "Sparsh" (touch) — reflecting our core belief that compassionate, therapeutic touch is essential to a mother's postpartum healing.

Our primary partnership model is B2B — we work directly with hospitals, maternity units, and nursing homes to integrate our services into their postpartum care offering. Individual mothers benefit from our services through their hospital's programme.

We partner with maternity units to offer our programmes as an add-on service for admitted postpartum mothers. Our therapists come to your facility on a scheduled basis, work within your ward environment, and follow your clinical protocols. There is no infrastructure investment required from the hospital.

We handle all therapist scheduling, equipment, and client communication. You benefit from enhanced patient satisfaction and improved recovery outcomes.

Clinical Questions

Key findings include: reduction in cortisol levels by up to 31%, improvement in oxytocin levels, 53% reduction in postpartum anxiety, 72% reduction in breast engorgement, and measurable improvement in pain scores and sleep quality.

Our protocols are designed in consultation with our clinical advisory board and are aligned with WHO Postpartum Care Guidelines (2022).

Yes, when performed correctly. Our LSCS programme is specifically designed for post-caesarean mothers. On Day 1 post-surgery, we only perform an assessment — no massage. From Day 2, we begin with gentle, low-pressure massage avoiding the surgical site completely. We progressively increase depth and coverage as healing progresses.

All sessions for LSCS mothers are conducted only after therapist assessment and with physician clearance as per hospital protocol.

Before each session, our therapists review the mother's medical notes and check for contraindications including: active infection or fever, blood clots (DVT), pre-eclampsia or eclampsia, open or infected wounds, haematoma, active bleeding, and any physician-indicated restrictions.

Any session where contraindications are present is postponed until clinical clearance is obtained.

Our breast massage and lactation support service is offered as an add-on to any programme. It involves gentle therapeutic massage targeting the breast tissue, Montgomery glands, and milk ducts to stimulate milk flow, relieve engorgement, and unblock ducts.

Sessions last 1–2 hours and include guidance on home care techniques for continued benefit. We do not replace lactation consultants — we complement their work as part of a holistic care team.

Our Therapists

All MaatriSparsh therapists complete a rigorous certification programme covering: postpartum anatomy and physiology, therapeutic massage techniques (Swedish, lymphatic drainage, deep tissue), clinical contraindications, infection control and hygiene in hospital settings, Ayurvedic marma therapy fundamentals, and lactation support techniques.

They also complete ongoing refresher training and clinical supervision. All therapists are background-checked before deployment.

The number of therapists assigned to your facility depends on your maternity unit's admission volume. We work with you during the onboarding process to calculate the appropriate team size and scheduling frequency. Typically, one therapist can serve 4–6 mothers per day.
