Our Vision

Every mother deserves to heal with dignity

In India, over 27 million births happen each year. Yet postpartum recovery — a mother's most vulnerable window — is rarely given the clinical attention it deserves. MaatriSparsh was founded to change that.

We believe recovery is not a luxury. It is a clinical necessity — and a mother's right. Our mission is to bring evidence-based, compassionate postpartum care into every maternity unit in India.
